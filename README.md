# The World Today — 23 November 2026

This repository is an entry for the make.fun bounty **Sum up 23 November's world news in one image** (`news-image-20261123`). It presents Monday 23 November 2026's headlines as a single 1024×1024 newspaper front page (`entry.png`). Running `main.py` copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` (or to a custom output directory passed as the first command-line argument), ensuring any two runs produce byte-identical output.

## Stories Pictured

- **COP31 collapse in Antalya, Türkiye** — The longest climate summit on record closes without a deal after the EU and island nations reject a final text omitting fossil fuels, sending unresolved questions on to COP32 in Addis Ababa.
- **Dymer apartment strike near Kyiv** — A drone strike collapses a residential block in the Kyiv region, leaving 14 people unaccounted for as rescuers search the rubble.
- **Cikande candy factory fire** — A blaze guts a confectionery plant in Cikande, Banten, with 50 missing and seven body bags recovered.
- **Hormuz** — Iran claims a strike on a U.S. base in Bahrain as a Saudi-led coalition intercepts a missile.
- **South Korea** — The education minister resigns amid an AI cheating scandal at the national exams.
- **Lisbon** — Thousands march across Portugal's capital over rising living costs.
- **Also Today** — Nepal PM Balendra Shah holds talks with Prime Minister Modi in New Delhi, and India post a record 720/9 declared in the Wellington Test.
